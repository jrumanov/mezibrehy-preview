Garden design

We design gardens that suit your plot, your way of living and your budget — from the first visit through to a finished planting plan.

  • A design made to measure

    A layout driven by the shape of the plot, its orientation, and how you intend to use the garden.

  • Species selection

    Plants that will genuinely thrive in the given position, accounting for soil, moisture and ongoing care.

  • Planting plan

    A document you can plant from in stages, as time and budget allow.

    We can carry out the design too, including plant material from our own supply.

Garden revitalization

An overgrown or neglected garden does not mean starting from scratch. Revitalization keeps what is worth keeping and transforms the rest.

  1. Assessing the garden

    We visit, assess the condition of the trees, soil and terrain, and agree what is worth keeping.

  2. Revitalization design

    We prepare a design for the transformation, including species selection and a phased schedule.

  3. Carrying out the work

    We remove what has had its day, treat what stays, and plant what is new.

  4. Aftercare

    We advise on caring for the garden so the transformation lasts — and can keep looking after it for you.
